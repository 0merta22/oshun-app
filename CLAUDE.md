Founder Background
DeAundre Webb is a former network engineer turned entrepreneur. His technical background informs Oshun's core operating principles: scalability, usability, functionality, purpose, and data-driven decision making. Oshun was born from a personal moment — watching his wife frustrated by having to stop everything to find hair products — and grew into a mission to serve a community that has been systematically underserved by the beauty industry.


PART 2 — THE OSHUN BRAND
Origin Story
The idea came from a moment at home. DeAundre's wife was flustered — she had to stop in the middle of her day to find hair products. He thought: "There should be something for that." He waited a year expecting someone else to build it. They didn't. So he did.
Mission Statement
To finally see what has gone unseen for far too long — and build the ecosystem Black beauty consumers have always deserved, with the unapologetic force of a movement that will not be ignored.
Brand Promise
"Why hasn't this been here all along?" That is the first thought every Oshun customer should have. Every design decision, every delivery window, every product listing, every notification must protect this feeling. Oshun should feel inevitable — like the thing that was always supposed to exist, and the world just finally had no choice but to catch up.
Official Tagline
"Dream Deep." Aquatic, ambitious, directive. It works on multiple levels — the ocean depth, the ambition of the vision, the community finally being seen. It tells the customer, the entrepreneur, and the kid in the workshop what Oshun expects of them.
Founder's Vision Statement
"I recognize what went unseen for far too long. The new wave is here — it's a vast Oshun out there." — DeAundre Webb
Legacy Statement
"It was all a dream." Every person Oshun is built for has said these words about something they wanted and weren't sure was possible. That's not a coincidence. That's the mission.

PART 5 — THE ECOSYSTEM
Oshun is the mother brand. Every vertical serves the same community, feeds the same ecosystem, and keeps wealth circulating where it belongs.

OSHUN (Mother Brand)

├── Oshun Marketplace     — Core on-demand delivery app

├── Oshun Desk            — CRM for beauty businesses

├── Oshun Health          — Organic wellness & beauty products

└── Oshun Foundation      — Technical workshops → certification → employment pipeline

The closed loop: The customer orders through Oshun. The shop manages it through Oshun Desk. The driver is part of the Oshun network. The graduate who built the feature learned at the Foundation. The money never has to leave.
Market Context
Black consumers contribute an estimated $9 billion annually to the beauty market
This demographic has been chronically underserved and underrepresented in beauty tech
No viable on-demand solution exists specifically for this community at scale
Oshun is not filling a gap — it is a declaration and a change of the guard
